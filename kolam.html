A kolam is one closed line, so circles can draw it too. Slide down: with few circles the loops melt into soft waves.

How to draw a kolam by hand
  1. Put down the dots in a square grid. Here the grid is turned on its corner, like a diamond.
  2. Start your line between two dots, never on one.
  3. Curve around a dot, then slip through the gap between two dots, always diagonally.
  4. When you reach the edge, or a turning point, swing back around the dot instead of going on.
  5. Keep going without lifting your hand until you are back where you started. Every dot ends up inside its own little loop.

Tip: turn on “Turning points” to see where the line has to turn back. Slide “Moment” to go through the drawing slowly.
