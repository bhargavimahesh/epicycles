Epicycle Ink

Any closed drawing can be made from spinning circles. Build shapes from circles, or draw your own and turn it into circles.

3 · Kolam

Each circle spins, and the next circle's centre rides on its edge. A pen on the last circle draws the shape.

Speed = turns per lap. A minus speed turns the other way.
